# Beat Gurus - Website & Business Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary
**Beat Gurus** is a prominent fusion percussion band based in **Bangalore, India**, founded by **Ganesh Govindswamy** and **Prashanth Muralidhar**. They specialize in West African percussion, primarily the **Djembe**, blended with Indian rhythms and cultural influences.

## 3. Services Offered
*Derived from external sources due to website incompleteness.*

Beat Gurus offers live musical entertainment for a variety of occasions:
*   **Corporate Events**: High-energy performances for company gatherings.
*   **Weddings & Private Parties**: Entertainment for receptions, cocktails, and parties.
*   **Cultural Festivals**: Performances at events like the Bangalore International Wine Festival, Ganesh Utsava, and October Fest.
*   **Workshops**: Djembe drumming workshops (conducted by Ganesh Govindswamy).
*   **International Tours**: They have performed in the USA, Europe, Singapore, Malaysia, and Norway.

## 4. Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
*   **The "Raw" Sound**: They renounce electronic instruments, focusing purely on the raw energy of acoustic percussion.
*   **Cultural Fusion**: A unique blend of **West African Djembe** beats with **Indian traditional rhythms** and film music.
*   **Energy**: Known for high-energy, interactive performances that engage the audience ("spellbound and yearning for more").
*   **Exotic Instruments**: Use of rare and distinct instruments not commonly seen in standard Indian bands.
